    The Forno way

    WE WAIT 72 HOURS.
    SO YOU DON'T HAVE TO.

    Great pizza starts long before it reaches the oven.

    At Forno 72, every batch of dough begins three days before service. Flour, water, salt and time do most of the work.

    After a 72-hour fermentation, each pizza is stretched by hand, topped with carefully selected ingredients and fired at extreme heat.

    No shortcuts. No frozen dough.
    Just pizza made the way we believe it should be.
